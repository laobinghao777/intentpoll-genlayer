INTERPRETATION IS NOT AUTHORIZATION

A suggested choice is still your decision.

Frozen options

The question and 2–6 options are committed with a snapshot hash. The creator cannot edit them later.

Voter-owned consent

Only your wallet can classify, confirm or withdraw its current ballot. The poll creator cannot confirm for you.

Auditable changes

A revision removes any old confirmation immediately. Earlier text and consent events remain visible in the ledger.

A testnet preference tool, not a verified-person election. The tally counts wallet addresses, not unique people. Text and addresses are public. The creator can close a poll immediately; there is no guaranteed voting window. This prototype supports 30 addresses and 3 text revisions per address. The seeded poll uses one builder wallet and synthetic preferences. No real community participation is claimed.
